Ownership: Flight Editor over Flight lighting, environment, effects, and renderer capability schemas.

Author background, environment lighting, fog, shadows, post-processing, and renderer settings through typed contributions with defaults, validation, and preview. Keep viewport-only visualization settings separate from exported scene settings.

Acceptance requires capability changes, unsupported settings, asset references, preview parity, defaults/reset, undo, YAML round trips, and renderer-specific diagnostics without host-specific document forks.
